The Journey

✦
2003

A star is born in Suwon

Shin Yu-na arrives on December 9, 2003. Years later she'll be the tallest, loudest, brightest maknae in K-pop — but first, schoolyard floorball champion.

2017–18

The pre-debut glow-up

Trainee Yuna flashes on screen in Mnet's Stray Kids survival show and SBS' The Fan, and eagle-eyed netizens immediately start asking: who is she?

✚Imageearly days
2019

DALLA DALLA — debut!

February 12: ITZY debuts with IT'z Different. "DALLA DALLA" breaks records, "ICY" freezes the summer, and 15-year-old Yuna owns every single frame.

✚Imagedebut era
2020–21

WANNABE energy, LOCO levels

The shoulder dance goes global, Not Shy rides out, and CRAZY IN LOVE's "LOCO" proves ITZY's teen-crush reign is only accelerating.

2022–23

SNEAKERS on, world tour on

CHECKMATE launches ITZY's first world tour. "SNEAKERS", "CHESHIRE" and "CAKE" stack up while Yuna becomes a CF and festival favorite.

✚Imagetour era
2024–now

BORN TO BE, made of GOLD

BORN TO BE, "UNTOUCHABLE", the glittering "GOLD" era and Girls Will Be Girls — Yuna keeps collecting eras like trophies. The story is still being written.
